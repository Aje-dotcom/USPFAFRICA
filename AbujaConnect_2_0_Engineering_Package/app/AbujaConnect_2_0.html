Data quality rules

Imported community seed records are marked unverified. Do not treat user-supplied coordinates, school names, ward labels or simulated IoT readings as confirmed public data.
  1. Do not mark verified without a dated source and named verifier.
  2. Record emergency and outpatient hours separately.
  3. Record capability (e.g. maternity, trauma, oxygen, lab) only when confirmed.
  4. Specify referral requirement and receiving facility when known.
  5. Reconfirm phone, hours and service availability on a schedule.
  6. Do not store patient names, diagnoses or sensitive case details in public directory records.
